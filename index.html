Frequently Asked Questions

How much protein should I eat daily?

Generally, aim for 0.8-1.2 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight. If you're very active or trying to build muscle, you may need 1.2-2.0 grams per kilogram.

Is it better to eat 3 large meals or 6 small meals?

Both approaches can work! The key is finding what fits your lifestyle and helps you maintain consistent energy levels.

Should I take supplements?

A balanced diet should provide most nutrients you need. However, some people may benefit from vitamin D, B12, or omega-3 supplements.
